### Common pitfalls and how to avoid them
- **No SSH or limited CLI tools**: build `vendor` and assets in CI and upload them.   [glimpse.sh](https://glimpse.sh/blog/hostinger-github-actions-automated-laravel-deployment)
- **File permissions**: ensure `storage` and `bootstrap/cache` are writable after deploy. Use `chown`/`chmod` in remote script.
- **Large uploads time out**: split uploads or use tarball approach as shown.
- **Migrations causing downtime**: run non-destructive migrations first; keep backups.
